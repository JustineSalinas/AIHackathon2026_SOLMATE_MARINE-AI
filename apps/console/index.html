HOW TO RUN A VOYAGE

  1. Set the two ports. Click the map to drop the departure pin, then the destination pin. The pathfinder replans on every change.
  2. Describe the vessel. Open the vessel panel and enter length, beam, draft, capacity and engine rating. Every fuel and speed figure is derived from these â€” defaults are a generic 120 t ferry.
  3. Choose the conditions. Pick a weather preset, or Live conditions at vessel position to pull the real sea state at the boat's coordinates.
  4. Add hazards. The obstacle and storm tools place avoidance zones. Click an armed tool again to put it away.
  5. Get under way. Enter the passenger count and target crossing time, and the console solves for the throttle that meets it.
Reading the numbers

Every recommendation comes from the optimiser, not from a language model. Where a value is unavailable the console shows a dash rather than a guess â€” a dash means the optimiser was not reached, and it is a real state worth reporting.

If the requested crossing time is faster than the hull can achieve in the current sea, the console says so and reports the time it can make instead.
